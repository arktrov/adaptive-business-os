## Status transitions

Research Complete → Approved+pass / On Hold+hold / Rejected+reject. Pairing is required in the prompt but not checked with a deterministic cross-field predicate. 03 separately requires Approved AND pass.

## Error handling

No error handlers. No Sources bundles can prevent the gate from receiving a package. With multiple stories, verify aggregator boundaries and bundle association from execution evidence.

## Retries / idempotency

No stage claim/checkpoint or gate-revision identity. Rerun can overwrite previous approved hook and guardrails; no captured immutable review history.

## Upstream scenarios

02 after Research Complete, with its persisted Sources projection.

## Downstream scenarios

03 gated by both H Approved and AD pass.

## Known special cases

The prompt requests research uncertainties and overclaim risks, but the upstream explicit arrays were not persisted. Narrative notes may retain some; completeness is not guaranteed. A safer approved_hook can replace the old hook. This is a pre-script gate, not final-video QA or publication clearance.

## Business logic to preserve

Fact safety above virality; authoritative/independent evidence; spot-check high-risk first/only/causal/numeric/date/identity/quote claims; no unsupported certainty; explicit blocking claims, corrections and enforceable script guardrails.

## Technical implementation to replace later

Immutable evidence-bound FactGuard decision, deterministic result/status pairing, persisted blockers and typed correction/guardrail arrays.

## Required validation

T05: status/result mismatch, multiple-story grouping, absent evidence, lost blocker IDs, corrections/guardrails through 03.
