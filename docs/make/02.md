## Purpose

Research individual claims, classify supporting sources, record uncertainty and recommend whether the evidence package can reach Fact Guard.

## Trigger

Story Pipeline H=Research Ready, limit 1, A1:DZ1; module 7 repeats status guard.

## Inputs

Story identity/framing/evaluation/notes, existing sources and factual summary plus narrative fields U:Z.

## Outputs

Claims expanded into Sources rows (one per claim×source URL), then research summary and recommended status written to the story. Structured model output also contains information not explicitly persisted.

## Google Sheets dependencies

Story Pipeline read/update; Sources append by named fields: Story ID, Claim / Fact, Claim ID, URL, Publisher, Source Type, Rights Status and Notes=qualifier. Source ID, confidence, Verified and needs_qualification are not mapped.

## Google Drive dependencies

None.

## Data structures

Strict ark_trov_research; claims[] {claim_id, claim, claim_type, confidence, source_urls[], needs_qualification, qualifier}; sources[] {source_id,url,title,publisher,source_type,rights_status}; uncertainty and overclaim arrays plus top-level research fields. Separate Make research ParseJSON structure; source joins use URL equality against model result.

## Status transitions

Research Ready → Research Complete / On Hold / Rejected from schema/instructions. Completion follows the claim-source append aggregator.

## APIs / providers

OpenAI Responses gpt-5.5, required web_search_preview, medium context/reasoning, max_output_tokens=6000, store=true, synchronous. Google Sheets.

## Error handling

No exception routes. Zero claims or zero source URLs may yield no aggregation/downstream update; verify Make empty-bundle behavior. Failure mid-append leaves a partial source package.

## Retries / idempotency

Append-only Sources, no per-claim/source uniqueness or in-progress lease. Replay may duplicate evidence rows. Claim IDs CLAIM-001… and source IDs SRC-001… are story-local sequences, not global keys.

## Upstream scenarios

External Research Ready promotion after 01.

## Downstream scenarios

02B consumes Research Complete plus Sources rows.

## Known special cases

The input prompt wrongly asks Research Ready/Needs Review/Rejected; the separate instructions and JSON enum correctly require Research Complete/On Hold/Rejected. This is a real prompt contradiction, not a change made during audit. Additional URL join expression differs from 00B (see contract appendix). uncertainty/overclaim arrays, claim confidence and source IDs are not directly stored, despite Fact Guard expecting complete evidence.

## Business logic to preserve

Primary-source priority, actual discovered URLs only, independent confirmation, date checking, separation of fact/inference/uncertainty, no causal overclaim. Confidence and source strength 0–100, claim qualification, preliminary rights classifications (never public accessibility=permission). Research Complete does not mean scripting or publish approval.

## Technical implementation to replace later

Normalized Claim↔SourceEvidence records with complete typed persistence, explicit research state decision and deduplicated writes; reconcile contradictory prompt versions before replacement.

## Required validation

T04: contradictory status prompt; empty evidence; partial retry; full uncertainty/qualification retention; story-local IDs; URL-join serialization.
