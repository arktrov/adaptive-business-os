## Purpose

Standalone copy of the Instagram insight collection tail of scenario 10.

## Trigger

GetUserMedia for the configured account, limit 1. Schedule/activation unknown; no publication event trigger.

## Inputs

First media ID, caption, media timestamp/product type/permalink/ig_id and its insight values.

## Outputs

One Platform Analytics observation per processed media bundle.

## Google Sheets dependencies

Platform Analytics append; no Content Calendar or publication checkpoint lookup.

## Google Drive dependencies

None.

## Data structures

Instagram media + aggregated name/values metrics. Date=media.timestamp, Content ID=media.id, Format=media_product_type, Title=caption, Instagram ID=ig_id, Post URL=permalink, Retrieved At=now.

## Status transitions

No story/production transition.

## APIs / providers

Instagram for Business GetUserMedia/GetMediaInsights and Google Sheets. Requests comments, ig_reels_avg_watch_time, ig_reels_video_view_total_time, likes, shares, total_interactions, views, reach, saved, reposts, reels_skip_rate.

## Error handling

No explicit fallback for unsupported metrics/media, missing observations or provider failure.

## Retries / idempotency

Every run appends; no unique media+checkpoint+retrieval key. Repeated latest-media collection and overlap with 10 may double-count.

## Upstream scenarios

Existing externally published Instagram media, no supplied publisher.

## Downstream scenarios

Platform Analytics only; learning/performance analysis not implemented in the complete set.

## Known special cases

Semantic comparison confirms modules 10/11/13/14 match scenario 10 in IDs, types, parameters, mappings and filters. Reported watch total divides by 60000, average by 1000 (configured ms→minutes/seconds conversion; provider payload units still require validation). Fetch limit 1, no explicit media-type filter or complete historical pagination.

## Business logic to preserve

Media/account association, requested metric identities, attribution to exact media and separate media date vs collection date. Preserve unsupported/missing status in the target rather than inventing zeros.

## Technical implementation to replace later

Shared Instagram adapter invoked through a single checkpoint owner; historical/published-media selection and deduplicated normalized observations. Disable/consolidate nothing in this audit.

## Required validation

T12: scenario10 overlap; non-Reel/unsupported metric; pagination; missing metric; units; repeated polls and collection-date versus publication-date.
