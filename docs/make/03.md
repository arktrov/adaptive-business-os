## Purpose

Convert an approved story into an English longform production package and two derived short scripts.

## Trigger

Story H=Approved AND AD=pass, limit 1, A1:BZ1; extra model guard checks Approved.

## Inputs

Approved hook, source URLs, story/research notes, evaluation, Fact Guard result/notes and explicitly AJ required corrections + AK script guardrails.

## Outputs

Production row with Production ID `PROD- {Story ID}`, Status=Script Drafted, 21 package fields, created/updated timestamps; original story moves to Script.

## Google Sheets dependencies

Read Story Pipeline; append Production Pipeline A:Z; update Story A:T mappings. No dedup lookup. Values beyond T in the story update are unmapped; actual clear/preserve semantics require validation.

## Google Drive dependencies

None.

## Data structures

Strict arktrov_production_package: final_title, alternative_titles, core_angle, opening_hook, full_script, script_word_count, scene_plan_json, visual_direction, thumbnail_concept, thumbnail_text, video_description, keywords, comment_question, cta, short_hook_1/script_1, short_hook_2/script_2, source_guardrails, fact_disclaimer, target_duration_min. scene_plan_json is an array encoded as a string; each scene needs scene_number, approximate_time, narration_purpose, visual, on_screen_text, source_note by prompt.

## Status transitions

Story Approved+pass → Script; new Production → Script Drafted. The two writes are separate, so a created production can coexist with an unadvanced story after a crash.

## APIs / providers

OpenAI Responses gpt-5.5, no tools/new research, max_output_tokens=12000, store=false; Google Sheets.

## Error handling

No explicit exception paths or code validation for inner scene JSON, word count or all guardrail compliance.

## Retries / idempotency

Repeated addRow after a failed Story update can duplicate the same logical Production ID. No idempotent upsert, claim or revision boundary.

## Upstream scenarios

02B Approved AND pass.

## Downstream scenarios

04 consumes short_script_1; 05 later plans Short 1. No longform or Short 2 production consumer exists in the complete set.

## Known special cases

Prompt primary product is 10–14min / about 1600–1900 words, two shorts ~100–150 words; conflicts with discovery 18–28s and later fixed ~55s plan. Alternative titles/keywords are ` | ` strings. Thumbnail text ≤4 words is prompt-only. General descriptions/thumbnails/CTA are publish preparation, not platform-ready release.

## Business logic to preserve

Guardrails > corrections > Fact Guard notes > older framing hierarchy; approved hook authoritative; no new unsupported facts/research; preserve uncertainty and attribution in every output including titles/thumbnails/shorts. Evidence-led English narration, varied progression, specific comment question and honest ending.

## Technical implementation to replace later

Versioned production variants with structured scenes and packaging, factual traceability, atomic production creation + story transition; do not silently drop longform or Short 2 outputs.

## Required validation

T06: guardrail priority in every output; nested JSON; script length; duplicate append after crash; Short 2/longform preservation and unmapped-column semantics.
